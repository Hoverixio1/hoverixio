Information We Collect

The specific information you are asked to provide, and the reasons why it is collected, are made clear at the point of collection:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, and (where provided or collected during account setup, support enquiries, or checkout) email address, as well as business or operational details you submit.

Payment Gateway SDKs & Third-Party Service Providers

Our mobile and web applications may integrate third-party payment gateway SDKs (including Razorpay and/or Cashfree) and hosting/infrastructure providers to process installment payments, verify transactions, prevent fraud, and deliver core app functionality. Third-party SDKs and service providers process data according to their respective privacy policies and security obligations, and we do not sell user personal information for cross-app behavioral advertising.
